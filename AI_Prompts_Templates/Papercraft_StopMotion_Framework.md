# Papercraft & Stop-Motion AI Generation Framework

## Overview
This prompt structure is optimized for video generation models (like Veo, Kling, and LTX-2.5) to achieve a consistent, tactile stop-motion aesthetic, specifically for paper-cut collages and claymation styles. This is highly effective for historical documentaries (Vox-style) or stylized commercial storyboards.

## The Prompt Formula
`[Subject/Action] + [Medium/Material] + [Lighting/Cinematography] + [Motion Style]`

### 1. Base Variables
- **Medium (Papercraft):** "multi-layered paper-cut diorama, textured cardstock, visible paper edges, pop-up book mechanics"
- **Medium (Claymation):** "plasticine stop-motion, tactile clay texture, subtle thumbprints, physical miniature set"
- **Lighting:** "dark cinematic lighting, dramatic studio micro-photography, rim light on edges, soft volumetric shadows"
- **Motion:** "12 fps stop-motion aesthetic, slightly jittery movement, tactile transitions"

### 2. Ready-to-Use Prompt Examples

**Example A: Historical Explainer (Vox Style)**
> "A multi-layered paper-cut diorama of a 1950s street scene. Textured cardstock figures moving in a 12 fps stop-motion aesthetic. Dark cinematic lighting with rim lights catching the edges of the paper. High-end macro photography, shallow depth of field, tactile collage style."

**Example B: Brand Mascot (Claymation)**
> "A plasticine stop-motion character interacting with a glowing 3D glassmorphism UI element. Tactile clay texture with subtle thumbprints. Dramatic studio micro-photography, soft volumetric shadows, 12 fps slightly jittery movement."
